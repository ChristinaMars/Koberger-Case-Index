On July 2, 2025, Brian Kohberger pled guilty to the murders of four University of Idaho 
students and to one count of burglary. Mr. Kohberger’s plea sharply changed the Sixth Amendment 
fair trial/First Amendment balance in this case and has rendered the primary justification for 
sealing records in this case moot . Indeed, the infringement on the First Amendment rights of the 
media  and public through the voluminous sealing of records in this case is no longer justifiable , 
nor does it comport with Idaho Court Administrative Rule 32.  
Rule 32 expressly allows for a “non -part[y] whose rights are affected by or who otherwise 
have a right to access information contained in a court file, may move to . . . unseal records in a case file.” I.C.A.R. 32(i)(1). Accordingly, a coalition of media organizations (the “Media Coalition ”
