 Last week, the Idaho Supreme Court held that a “vague, overb road, unduly restrictive, or 
not narrowly drawn” gag order is “an unconstitutional obstacle to [Intervenors] gathering” 
information about this case. In re Petition for Writ of M andamus or Writ of Prohibition , No. 50482, 
2023 WL 3050829, at *5 (Idaho Apr. 24, 2023). But on procedural grounds, the Idaho Supreme 
Court decided that this Court should have a chan ce, in the first instance , to vacate the amended 
nondissemination order entered January 18, 2023 (the “Gag Order”). Following the Idaho Supreme Court’s guidance, and the for the reasons provide d in the accompanying me morandum, Intervenors 
now ask this Court to vacate the Gag Order because  it is vague, overbroad, unduly restrictive, and 
not narrowly drawn. DATED:  May 1, 2023.
