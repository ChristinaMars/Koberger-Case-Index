IN LIMINE # 3 RE: USE OF THE TERM MURDER       Page 2    Mr. Kohberger’s  motion seeks to exclude the State, in its case presentation, from using 
any form of the word murder as it applies to him .  His request is based in his Constitutional Rights 
to a fair trial and due process of law as well as the Idaho Rules of Evidence 403.1  Mr. Kohberger, 
in his motion specifically stated that it was not the use of the word murder but of phrasing of the 
word that labeled him.   
 Mr. Kohberger has maintained his innocence since the day he was accused.  His objection  
is about referring to him as a murderer.  To allow the State to present its case by labeling him as a 
murderer is improper and inflammatory.  That will be the ultimate jury question, and the jury must 
not be prejudiced by making a decision based on influence, labels and inference of what the State 
opines .
