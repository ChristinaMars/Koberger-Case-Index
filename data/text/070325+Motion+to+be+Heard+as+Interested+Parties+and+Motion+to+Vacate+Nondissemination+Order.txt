Interested Parties. 
 
On July 2, 2025, Brian Kohberger pled guilty to the murders of four University of Idaho 
students and to one count of burglary. Mr. Kohberger’s plea sharply changed the Sixth Amendment 
fair trial/First Amendment balance in this case. At the request of the State, and with agreement of 
Mr. Kohberger, the Court left the Nondissemination Order in place until Mr. Kohberger’s 
sentencing, which is set for July 23, 2025. However, the Nondissemination Order, and its 
infringement on the First Amendment rights of the media, is no longer justifiable and should be 
vacated immediately.  
Accordingly, a coalition of media organizations, including American Broadcasting 
Companies, Inc., d/b/a/ ABC News; the Associated Press; Bonner County Daily Bee; CBS News; 
CNN; Cowles Publishing, d/b/a/ The Spokesman-Review; Coeur d’Alene Press; Daily Mail; 
EastIdahoNews.com, LLC, d/b/a/ East Idaho News; FOX News; Idaho Press Club; Ion Media 
Networks, LLC, d/b/a Court TV; KXLY; Lewiston Tribune/TPC Holdings Inc.; Moscow-Pullman 
Daily News/TPC Holdings Inc.; Scripps Media, Inc., d/b/a/ KIVI-TV; Society of Professional 
Journalists; States Newsroom, d/b/a/ Idaho Capital Sun; TEGNA; The McClatchy Company, LLC, 
d/b/a The Idaho Statesman; The New York Times Company; NBC News; NewsNation; and

129532183.1 0099999-00006  University of Idaho Student Media/The Argonaut (the “Media Coalition”)1 respectfully move this 
Court for leave to file their Motion to Vacate the Nondissemination Order filed contemporaneously 
herewith. See The Associated Press v. Second Jud. Dist. , 172 Idaho 113, 121, 529 P.3d 1259, 1267 
(2023) (“both state and federal courts often permit the media to intervene in criminal cases on a 
limited basis—or at least file a motion as interested parties—in the defense of public access and 
free speech, including in Idaho.”). The Media Coalition further requests the opportunity to present 
oral argument on this issue at the Court’s earliest available opening. This Motion is supported by 
the accompanying memorandum, the Declaration of Rebecca Boone in Support of Motion to be 
Heard and Vacate Nondissemination Order, and Declaration of Wendy J. Olson in Support of 
Motion to be Heard and Vacate Nondissemination Order.
